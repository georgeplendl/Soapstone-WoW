A gacha game is a type of video game that incorporates a mechanic similar to gachapon (capsule toy vending machines) found in Japan. In these games, players spend in-game currency or real money to obtain random virtual items, characters, or upgrades. These items are often categorized by rarity, with more powerful or desirable items being less common.

Key features of gacha games include:

1. **Randomized Rewards**: Players receive random items or characters from a pool, often with varying levels of rarity and power.
2. **In-Game Currency**: Typically, there are two types of currency: one that can be earned through gameplay and another that can be purchased with real money.
3. **Microtransactions**: Players can spend real money to buy more chances at obtaining rare items or characters.
4. **Collection Element**: Many gacha games encourage players to collect a wide variety of characters or items, often with unique abilities or attributes.
5. **Progression Systems**: These games often include systems for leveling up characters, enhancing their abilities, and forming teams for strategic gameplay.

Popular examples of gacha games include "Genshin Impact," "Fate/Grand Order," and "Fire Emblem Heroes." While these games can be enjoyable and engaging, they have also been criticized for their potential to encourage excessive spending, leading to comparisons with gambling. As a result, some regions have implemented regulations to ensure transparency and fairness in gacha mechanics.
