## Cairns — Location-Based Pin Skin Pickups

**Cairns** are special map markers placed at real-world points of interest (landmarks, parks, museums, murals, historic sites). The name references stacked trail-marker stones — fitting both the exploration ethos of Soapstone and its Dark Souls inspiration.

---

### Core Mechanic

- Walk within range of a Cairn → tap it → receive a **pin skin pull** from that Cairn's themed pool
- Each Cairn has a **cooldown** (e.g., 24 hours) before the same user can pull from it again
- The skin pool is **location-themed** — a Cairn at a waterfront gives aquatic/ripple skins, a Cairn at a historic site gives aged/weathered stone skins, a Cairn at a park gives overgrown/mossy skins

Pin skins are cosmetic. They change how your dropped stones appear as pins on the map for all other users.

---

### Why It Works for Soapstone

- Mirrors the core reason people drop interesting stones — they're somewhere worth recording. Now there's a reward for *going* to cool spots even if you have nothing to say.
- Cairn markers become a breadcrumb trail that naturally surfaces interesting places in a city to new users.
- Stone pins naturally cluster near Cairns, making the map richer and more active at real-world hotspots.
- Similar to how Pokémon GO uses PokéStops at real-world landmarks to encourage physical exploration.

---

### Cairn Tiers

| Tier | Example Locations | Pull Pool |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Parks, plazas, transit hubs | Basic glyph/shape skins |
| Notable | Museums, monuments, local landmarks | Animated or glowing pin skins |
| Legendary | Major landmarks, rare or hard-to-reach spots | Unique location-stamped skins — only obtainable at that specific Cairn |

**Legendary skins** are a status flex — other users see a pin on the map and know the dropper was physically at that specific place. A skin tied to the Space Needle, the Brooklyn Bridge, or a remote mountain trail becomes a trophy.

---

### Community Nomination (Long-Term)

Users could nominate new Cairn locations, similar to Pokéstop submissions in Pokémon GO (originally via Ingress Waypoints). For launch, seed a curated set of Cairns manually per city and let the community grow the network over time.
