## Initial Concept
- As easy as snapchat
- Instagram location tagging
- You just walk to a place, open app, hold down a button, talk, let go of button
- Auto play in order, easily can skip or down vote, can easily mark a voice user as ignore so you'll never hear their voice notes again
- See voice dots on a map and GPS navigate to them
## Ideas
- [[Idea - Gatcha Gamification]]
- Achievements / Gowalla & Foursquare badges
	- "Dropped 100 Soapstones"
	- "Dropped Soapstones in 50 different cities"
- Speech to text as you listen (ala Spotify lyrics)
- Layers (ie. subreddits/genre based voice notes)
- Offline mode/no data service on your device. Enables recording without an internet connection. Sync data when device is back online.
- Geofencing alerts - Alerts users when they enter or leave a specific location with an voice note. No autoplaying, just a notification.
- Integration with wearables - Support for smart watches or other wearables to record and play messages on the go. Use haptic feedback to notify users of nearby messages.
- Heatmaps on map (maybe when zoomed out for quickly seeing where users are in your city without letting them listen to just anything anywhere at anytime on the globe)
- Customizable voice note pins/icons that you can add to your voice note. Maybe behind a paywall or a part of the previously stated [[Idea - Gatcha Gamification]] idea?
- Likes, upvotes, downvotes, text comments associated with a voice message.
- Photo associated with a voice message
	- Probably a bad idea, but would be fun.
- Trending areas -  If there is a big event and we see an area with a lot of messages being posted in an area, you can let localish users know via notification and let them listen in on whats going on.
- React to messages with emoji ala Discord (Casey idea)
- Automatically transcribe to text and index for search (Lyle idea)
- Driving mode interface, swipe right for like and next, left for dislike, up for next, down for previous (Lyle Idea)
- Tagging voice recordings that are detected to be recorded via a fast moving car as "drive-by's"
- What if an image must ALWAYS be added to a voice message recording?
- Yelp-like reactions to voice notes (Helpful(14), Funny(3), Useful, etc.)
- Bookmarks - Save a recording and place so you can always go back to it/navigate to it.
- Associate a business name to location on map if detected to be accurately inside 
- Unlock more features to the app like image posting to users behind an updoot count threshold
- Allow MMS voice memos based submissions from your phones text app (Justin Idea)
- Ability to have a "Tour" where users can generate their own start and finish points. Only a single users voice messages along the way (ability to mute others users or not) and integration into Google Maps for navigation directions
- Tags (Matt idea)
- Localized ads (Matt idea)
- Sponsored map dots w/ voice messages from the brand/company/business ala Google Maps
	- Ads removed via paypig plan (Matt idea)
- Free users can listen with limited # of posts, Tier 1 sub can post 10 seconds, Tier 2 sub can post 10+ seconds (limit?) & images after updoot threshold.
- Polls?
- No log policy (Similar to Mullvad or Signal)
- No usernames (Matt idea)
- Random user names (Juicy Pigeon, Sleepy Skunk, Running Yak, etc)
- Ignore certain users - Ban them from ever showing up on voice notes or the map
- Karma for users - Updoot count with gives them some privileges
- Notification to remind users to drop a voice note for when you leave a restaurant to see what you liked on the menu.
- Bounties
- Log of whats been listened to and how many times.
- Add a vote threshold filter and ratio filter for autoplay or shown posts (Justin idea)
- If users can Mark posts as " toxic ", after a certain threshold, they can be a setting to show no toxic posts on your map
