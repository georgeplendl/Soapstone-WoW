SOAPSTONE COMPANION: PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING

The Soapstone companion shares the stones you leave in World of Warcraft
with other Soapstone players, through a shared server.

WHAT IT DOES TO YOUR PC
- Installs for your Windows user only (no admin rights needed).
- Starts with Windows and runs in the system tray. You can turn this off
  any time: right-click the tray icon and untick "Start with Windows".
- Installs and updates the Soapstone addon in your WoW Forever folder, and
  writes a small helper addon next to it (Interface\AddOns\SoapstoneData).
  It never touches your SavedVariables, other addons, or the game itself.
- Keeps its settings and a cache in %APPDATA%\Soapstone.

WHAT IT SENDS TO THE SOAPSTONE SERVER
- Your characters' names, the stones you leave (words or drawing, where in
  the game, and when), your edits and deletions, appraisals, reports, and
  which stones your characters have opened.
- A random install id and token it makes up itself. No account, email or
  password.

WHO CAN SEE IT
- Everyone using Soapstone sees the stones you leave, with your character's
  name, where you left them and when.
- Only your own companion gets back which stones your characters opened.
- Your IP address is only kept as a keyed hash, for rate limits.

UNINSTALLING
- Windows Settings > Apps > Soapstone > Uninstall. This removes the program
  and its "Start with Windows" entry.
- To remove the rest, delete %APPDATA%\Soapstone, and in your game folder
  Interface\AddOns\SoapstoneData (and Interface\AddOns\Soapstone if you no
  longer want the addon).
